Discover CEDRUMO
Recognise

Scan and recognise your cigar

Simply photograph the cigar band and CEDRUMO takes care of the rest. The app scans the cigar and suggests the brand, series or line, country of origin, blend with blend notes, along with matching aromas and tasting notes. You enter the format yourself, it is not detected automatically. Then a quick check, confirm, done.

Three free cigar scans. From the fourth scan onwards, CEDRUMO Premium includes up to 50 AI scans per month. You can try Premium free for seven days.

Manage

Manage your collection

Manage up to three humidors or custom storage locations. CEDRUMO shows you the inventory and value of each location as well as your entire collection. When something is running low, you can immediately see what needs to be replenished. Structured, clear and always at hand.

Remember

Preserve your moments of enjoyment

Record which cigar you enjoyed, when you enjoyed it and for what occasion – including the rum pairing, your rating and personal notes. After all, every cigar is enjoyed only once, but some stories remain.

Evening Planner · Premium

Plan your perfect evening with CEDRUMO

The Evening Planner combines occasion, mood, time, cigar preferences, drink pairing and other personal criteria to create a tailored recommendation for your enjoyment moment. Instead of choosing at random, CEDRUMO helps you bring the right cigar, the right pairing and the right atmosphere together.

From the beta

Tested in beta

Before launch, CEDRUMO was extensively tested in a closed beta by real cigar enthusiasts. Their feedback went directly into the app's development, from cigar recognition to simple everyday use.

97% of scanned cigars were correctly identified, 66% of them spot on in the very first round. The second round brought a clear, noticeable improvement. Remaining inaccuracies mainly concerned the country of origin. An object deliberately tested that was not a cigar was correctly rejected as such.
