Explore the features first
Recognise

Recognize your cigar

Simply photograph the cigar band – CEDRUMO takes care of the rest. The app recognizes the cigar, provides details such as the brand, line and origin, and suggests matching aromas and tasting notes. You enter the cigar's format individually. Review, confirm – done.

Manage

Manage your collection

Manage up to three humidors or custom storage locations. CEDRUMO shows you the inventory and value of each location as well as your entire collection. When something is running low, you can immediately see what needs to be replenished. Structured, clear and always at hand.

Remember

Preserve your moments of enjoyment

Record which cigar you enjoyed, when you enjoyed it and for what occasion – including the rum pairing, your rating and personal notes. After all, every cigar is enjoyed only once, but some stories remain.

Evening Planner · Premium

Plan your perfect evening with CEDRUMO

The Evening Planner combines occasion, mood, time, cigar preferences, drink pairing and other personal criteria to create a tailored recommendation for your enjoyment moment. Instead of choosing at random, CEDRUMO helps you bring the right cigar, the right pairing and the right atmosphere together.
