Delete your account and data

How to delete your CEDRUMO account (the "Cigar Collection Manager" app) and all associated data.

Deletion directly in the app

You can delete your user account and all associated cloud data yourself at any time: open the app, go to Settings and select the option to delete your account. The deletion runs immediately and removes your account and content from the active systems.

Note: merely uninstalling the app does not delete the data stored in the cloud. For a complete deletion, please use the deletion function in Settings.

What is deleted

  • your pseudonymous user account (anonymous user ID) and its sign-in record
  • your stored content: cigars, rums, evening-planner entries and humidor entries, along with the ratings, favourites and notes they contain
  • your uploaded photos
  • your Premium status and the associated in-app subscription mapping

For legal reasons (proof of deletion under Art. 30 GDPR), a minimal deletion record is retained. It contains only the anonymous identifier and the time of deletion, and none of your content.

Active subscription

An active subscription is managed through your Google Play account. Deleting your app account does not automatically cancel a running subscription. Please also cancel an active subscription directly in Google Play under "Payments and subscriptions".

Retention after deletion

After deletion, the data is removed from the active systems. To protect against data loss, deleted database states may be retained in backups for a limited time: up to 7 days through point-in-time recovery and up to 14 days in the daily database backups; deleted images up to 7 days through the soft-delete function. After these periods expire, the data is deleted automatically and permanently. If a backup is restored, deletions that have already been completed are reapplied using the deletion log before the data is returned to use.

Automatic deletion after inactivity

Independently of this, the user account and its associated content are automatically deleted after 12 months of inactivity.

Deletion without access to the app

If you no longer have access to the app, you can also request the deletion of your data by email to info@cedrumo.com. Further information can be found in our privacy notice.
